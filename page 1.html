About the Topic

Jeffrey Dahmer was an American serial killer whose crimes became one of the most widely documented criminal cases in modern American history.

This website presents a factual overview of his early life, criminal case, and the investigation that eventually led to his arrest and conviction.

Why I Chose This Topic

I selected this topic because the case is an important example of how criminal investigations, law enforcement, and the justice system work. The website focuses on documented events rather than graphic descriptions of violence.
