Overview of the Case

Jeffrey Dahmer committed multiple murders between 1978 and 1991. Many of his crimes occurred in Milwaukee, Wisconsin.

The case became widely known after his arrest in July 1991, when investigators discovered evidence connecting him to numerous victims.

Note: This page intentionally avoids graphic descriptions and focuses on the documented legal and investigative aspects of the case.

General Timeline

1978

Dahmer committed his first known murder.

1980s

Additional murders occurred during the following years.

1991

Dahmer was arrested in Milwaukee after evidence connecting him to multiple murders was discovered.

1992

Dahmer was convicted and sentenced to multiple consecutive life terms in prison.

Location Connected to the Case

Milwaukee, Wisconsin became strongly associated with the case because many of the crimes investigated occurred there.
