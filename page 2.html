Early Life and Background

Jeffrey Lionel Dahmer was born on May 21, 1960, in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. He grew up in a family that later moved to Ohio.

During his adolescence, Dahmer experienced difficulties in his personal and family life. Accounts of his childhood and teenage years have been examined extensively in biographies, court records, and documentaries.

Important Periods

Childhood

Dahmer spent his early childhood in Wisconsin before his family moved to Ohio.

Teenage Years

His teenage years included increasing social difficulties and troubling behavior that later became part of accounts examining his background.

Young Adulthood

As a young adult, Dahmer's life became increasingly unstable before his later crimes were discovered.
